# Marks

`logo.svg` is the European emblem at its required proportions. `icon.svg` is
the same emblem, untouched, centred on a square canvas for use as an icon.

## Drawing it

The emblem is drawn to the official graphics specification and must not be
redrawn by eye:

- the fly (width) is one and a half times the hoist (height);
- the field is Reflex Blue `#003399`, the stars are Yellow `#FFCC00`;
- twelve five-pointed stars, not touching, their centres on a circle whose
  radius is one third of the hoist;
- each star is circumscribed by a circle whose radius is one eighteenth of the
  hoist;
- the stars sit at the twelve hour positions, and **one point of every star
  points vertically upward**, whatever its position on the circle. Rotating the
  stars to face the centre is the usual mistake.

Never restretch, recolour, rotate, crop or add to it. If an icon needs a square,
place the emblem on a square canvas as `icon.svg` does; do not deform it.

## The conditions we accept in using it

Use of the emblem by third parties is governed by the administrative agreement
published at OJ C 271, 8.9.2012 (2012/C 271/04). It permits use by any natural
or legal person, commercial or not, **unless** the use creates the incorrect
impression of a connection with an EU institution, or leads the public to
believe the user benefits from its support, sponsorship, approval or consent.
It does not permit registering the emblem, or an imitation of it, as a trade
mark or any other IP right.

This project therefore:

1. states the absence of affiliation and endorsement immediately beside the
   emblem, in the readme and on any site that carries it;
2. claims no trade mark or other IP right over the emblem or any part of it;
3. **never places the emblem on a generated evidence pack.** The readme is the
   project speaking about itself; a pack is what a manufacturer hands to a
   market surveillance authority, and an emblem there would read as an official
   certification of the very thing the tool refuses to assert. A test enforces
   this.

Point 3 is the one that matters most, and it is the one easiest to undo by
accident. Do not add the emblem to the renderers.
