# Marks

`icon.svg` is the icon (favicon, small sizes). `logo.svg` and `logo-dark.svg`
are the full mark. Both are a citation bracket enclosing a cited line, which is
what the tool does: it points at a locus in the Official Journal text.

## What the mark deliberately is not

**Not a tick, a shield, a seal or a badge.** Those read as approval. The tool
prepares evidence and states no legal conclusion, so a mark that looks like a
verdict would contradict the product on sight.

**Not the European emblem, and not an imitation of it.** Use of the emblem by
third parties is governed by the administrative agreement published at OJ C 271,
8.9.2012 (2012/C 271/04). It permits use, commercial or not, *unless* the use
creates the incorrect impression of a connection with an EU institution, or
leads the public to believe the user benefits from its support, sponsorship,
approval or consent. A tool that prepares regulatory conformity evidence is
precisely the case the agreement excludes, and the emblem may not be registered
as a trade mark either. No circle of stars, and no arrangement that reads as
one.

The blue is the institutional register, not the flag: a colour carries no
protection, and the amber is deliberately far from the emblem's yellow so the
two are never seen as a pair.
